Gravel Gas: Dirt Bike Hills

Hit the gas, pull a wheelie and fly over the next hill. Gravel Gas is a 2D dirt bike game with wonky, bouncy physics that is easy to learn and hard to put down. In Danish it is called Grusgas: Motocross på bakker.

  • Gas tilts you back, the brake tilts you forward: pull off flips and nail perfect landings
  • 5 machines (dirt bike, quad, buggy, monster truck, rocket bike) and 5 worlds
  • 30 secret things fly past, one every 1,000 m
  • Daily missions, chests, a new event every week and a season track
