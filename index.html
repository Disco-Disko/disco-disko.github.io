About

Why "Disko"?

Disco is spelled disko in Danish.
So the name is a typo a wink.

Disco Disko is a one-person studio run from Denmark. One person draws the hills, writes the physics, picks the sounds and answers the support mail. That keeps the games small and personal, and it means that when you write to us, you're writing to the person who made it.

The first game, Gravel Gas, is finished and on its way to the App Store and Google Play. More small, silly games are sketched out after that.
